EMBER COURT
A three-lane card duel against the Warden

Goal: reduce the Warden's life to 0, or hold more life after 10 rounds.

Each round you gain ember equal to the round (max 8) and draw up to 5 cards. Unspent ember carries over (max 10) — save up for a big round, or spend it now. The first player alternates each round; you start round 1.

Play cards into the three lanes — Ember Gate, Frost Bridge, Thorn Hollow. A card costs ember; its power adds to your side of that lane.

Lanes resolve when you end the round: the stronger side in a lane deals the difference as damage to the other court. Sappers remove 3 enemy power; Banners give +1 to every other friendly card in their lane.

Win by taking the Warden to 0 life. Lose yours, and the court is his.

Original game · plays fully offline · no account, no network
